📘 Platform User Guide

1. What is Flash Assistant (O18)?

This platform replaces spreadsheets and chats for complex doubts and policies. It ensures a transparent, real-time workflow between Agents and the QA team.

2. How to submit a ticket

  • Enter the exact Job ID Number in the first step.
  • (Optional) In the second step, paste text or screenshots of the User Query and Model Response.
  • Open the dropdown and select Your Policy Hypothesis.
  • Write a clear Rationale explaining the context of your decision.
  • Click the button.

Understanding the Live Queue

The Doubts Queue is a "Live-Sync" table. It automatically updates every 4 seconds.

Color Codes (Pulsing Dots)

  • Yellow: Pending QA review.
  • Purple: Parked for further investigation.
  • Green: Safe / No Violation.
  • Red: Violation.

Meet Jixy, your Knowledge Bot

Jixy is a built-in Natural Language Processing (NLP) assistant programmed to help you retrieve official documents instantly.

  • Click the floating Golden Robot icon in the bottom right.
  • Type a simple keyword in the chat.
  • You can also submit bug reports or feedback to the developer by writing "Feedback" to Jixy.
